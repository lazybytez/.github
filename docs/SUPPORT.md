# Support

## Community help

The fastest way to get help from the community and maintainers is to join our [Discord server][discord].

## Bug reports and feature requests

Use the issue templates provided in the relevant repository. Before opening an issue, search for your subject to avoid creating a duplicate. Issues without a matching template will be closed as invalid.

## Security issues

Do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities. See our [Security Policy][security] for how to report them responsibly.

## General contact

For anything else, reach us at [contact@lazybytez.de][contact-email].
